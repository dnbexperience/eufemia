Third-party materials keep their own licenses. The Eufemia license does not replace those terms or grant rights DNB does not hold. Before using a font or another separately licensed asset, confirm that its applicable license or agreement permits your intended use, including embedding, self-hosting and redistribution where relevant. Inclusion in Eufemia is not proof of permission.

For confirmation of approved font use in a DNB Group project, contact the Eufemia team in [#eufemia-web](https://dnb-it.slack.com/archives/CMXABCHEY) or through the [contact page](/about/contact/). This includes DNB Sans, which Eufemia loads by default, and fonts bundled with individual themes. The team can help identify the applicable agreement and rights holder.
