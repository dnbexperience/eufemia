Choose the collection that matches your task. DNB brand logos and company or partner logos have different sources and usage guidance.

## DNB brand logos

### Brand Center

**Note:** If you have access to DNB's Brand Center, [you can find the logos under "Visuell identitet"](https://bc.dnb.no/brandcenter/no/dnbvisualguidelines/component/default/761).

### Brandbook

From the Brandbook PDF file, digital media should use the 3rd level logos (page 9).

## Company and partner logos

Eufemia maintains a shared library of company logos for use in DNB applications. The library includes light and dark variants where needed, and both logo and avatar formats when they are available.

The collection includes:

- Authentication logos, such as Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint symbols
- Company and product logos, such as Vipps
- Logos used across DNB products and partner integrations

[Browse the Eufemia Logos catalog](https://eufemia-logos.pages.dnb.ghe.com/) (DNB sign-in required).

[Browse the public company-logo manifest](https://assets.eufemia.dnb.no/logos/manifest.json), which points to the current immutable collection.

## Developers

For the DNB brand logo in a Web application, use the [Logo component](/web/components/logo/).

For company and partner logos, use the standalone catalogue or public manifest above. React applications use the `@eufemia/logos` package. The manifest points to immutable, versioned PNG assets. See the [Asset CDN documentation](/web/ai-tools-and-resources/asset-cdn/#company-logos) for public URLs and versioning.
