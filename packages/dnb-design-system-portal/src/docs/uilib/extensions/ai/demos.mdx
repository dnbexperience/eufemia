## Examples

### Full chat experience

A complete chat with an assistant. Choose a suggestion or write a message, e.g. "Block my card" or "Compare my spending", to see the reasoning, tools, answer and sources stream in. Press stop to end the reply, or regenerate the last answer.

The demo uses scripted replies and local chat state. Connect your own messages, status, send, stop and regenerate actions to the components in your app.
