### Interactive Cards

Use `Card.Action` to make a Card clickable or a link. Pass `onClick` for a button-like card, or `href`/`to` for a link card. Both variants get hover, focus, and keyboard styles automatically.

For custom routing (e.g. react-router), pass a router `Link` via the `element` prop on `Card.Action`.

<Examples.InteractiveCards />

### Info card layout

Use `Card.Info` directly for information and tips. Compose its content using existing components.

<Examples.InfoLayout />

### Info card actions

Set `onClick`, `href`, or `to` on Card.Info when the whole card performs one action or navigates to a URL. Keep the content free of nested links, buttons, and other interactive elements.

<Examples.InfoCardActions />

### Closable info card

Compose Card.Info.CloseButton inside Card.Info and wrap the card in [HeightAnimation](/web/components/height-animation/) to animate dismissal. HeightAnimation removes the card after the closing animation completes. In your application, move focus to a suitable remaining control when dismissing focused content.

### Image beside content

Set `innerSpace={0}` on Card.Info for edge-to-edge media and add padding around the content. Flex wraps the image above the text when the card becomes narrow, including inside a grid. Use descriptive `alt` text for meaningful images and `alt=""` for decorative images.

<MediaExamples.ImageCard />

### Centered image card

Set `maxWidth` directly on Card.Info and use Flex to center the content. Round the image's top corners without clipping the card, so keyboard focus outlines remain visible.

<MediaExamples.CenteredImageCard />

### Full media with video

Use a muted background video for decoration and keep all information in the card's text. The video plays automatically unless the user prefers reduced motion. Enabling reduced motion pauses playback; the user can still choose to play it. The keyboard-accessible control toggles play and pause without resetting playback. Use the first video frame as the poster to avoid a visual jump when playback starts. A poster image and the call to action remain available if playback fails.

Replace the video and poster URLs with your own media. `Card.Info.Overlay` supplies the gradient, progressive blur, text shadows for headings and paragraphs, padding, and dark-surface context for its controls. Check text contrast across the full video. For video that conveys information, provide native controls, captions, and a transcript; see [images and video](/web/components/card/#images-and-video).

Use `Card.Info.MediaButton` and pass the video ref. It handles playback state, reduced motion, translated labels, and the play/pause icon transition. Keep source, poster, and error fallback handling with the video.

The close control pauses playback, dismisses the card, and moves focus to this section's heading.
