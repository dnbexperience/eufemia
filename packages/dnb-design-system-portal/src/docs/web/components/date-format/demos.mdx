### Animated dates

Use `animate` to highlight a date change. Digits within each changing numeric field cascade from left to right, 25 ms apart with a 160 ms slide per digit.
Move one day at a time to try the month and year boundaries.

Month names and other text change immediately. Initial values and formatting
changes appear without motion. The animation respects reduced motion, and
screen readers receive the final date. Relative times and durations are not
animated.

Right-to-left dates use native text without animation to preserve shaping and reading order.

<Examples.Animated />

The animation styles are included in the standard Eufemia stylesheets. When
loading individual styles, include:

```tsx
import '@dnb/eufemia/fragments/animated-digits/style/dnb-animated-digits.min.css'
```

### Duration formatting

The DateFormat component automatically detects and formats ISO
8601 duration strings. No additional properties are needed.

- `PT1H` = 1 hour (P = period, T = time, 1H = 1 hour)
- `PT2H30M` = 2 hours 30 minutes
- `P1D` = 1 day (P = period, 1D = 1 day)
- `P1DT2H30M` = 1 day 2 hours 30 minutes
- `P1W` = 1 week,
- `P1M` = 1 month
- `P1Y` = 1 year
