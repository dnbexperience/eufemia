## Explore a desktop article

Scroll inside the savings guide to follow the animated selection outline through three heading levels. The TOC stays on the right below a sticky header. stickyOffset and currentSectionOffset share the header clearance, while scroll-padding-top keeps linked headings visible. This example uses the translated default title, “On this page”. Link clicks scroll only this example. On small screens, the TOC becomes a disclosure above the article.
