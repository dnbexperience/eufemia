## Please contribute

Eufemia is a [living design system](/web/about-the-lib/living-system) that does not have a "finished" state. Continuous improvement, removal, and addition of content are essential to keeping it relevant as a resource for current and future DNB products and services.

Your input, comments, and discussions are all valuable. Please [reach out to us](/design-system/contact) and [contribute](/contribute).
